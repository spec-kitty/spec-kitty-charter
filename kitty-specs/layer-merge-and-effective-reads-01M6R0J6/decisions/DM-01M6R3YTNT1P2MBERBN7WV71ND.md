## Question

Does an active enhancer alone satisfy a required T whatever keeps T out of force?

## Options

- Yes, always
- Only when a deny entry keeps T out
- Other

## Final answer

No. Enhancers always require their T to be active in order to work; they do not stand on their own. A required T is satisfied only by T itself or an active override of it; an enhancer never satisfies it, and an enhancer whose T is not active has no effect.
