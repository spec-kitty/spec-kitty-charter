## #408: cascade controls (ADR-0059 D2 amendment)

Source: the ruling comment on issue #408 (2026-11-07).

1. **The write cascade keeps the five relations plus `enhances`, with no depth limit, as ADR-0059 D2 ruled, and gains
   a `depth` parameter for iterative deepening.** A cascading select (`SelectionRequest`) and a cascading deactivate
   (`ExclusionRequest`) take `depth`. Without it the walk stays unlimited (core parity).
   - **Runner's call (decision `DM-01M6TDH0SY5Q224GX709DXWG8K`):** `depth` counts **`suggests` hops only**, as
     ADR-0018 D4's `depth` does for the action-context walk. `requires`, the other hard relations, `enhances` and the
     lineage pass-through are walked in full; `depth: 0` follows no `suggests` edge. Truncating a hard relation would
     write a selection whose requirements are not selected, and the breadth #408 reports comes from `suggests`, whose
     `when` a write cannot evaluate. One meaning of `depth` across the contract.
   - **Delivery:** PR A0, as WP30 (after WP08). It changes the request of #399's built cascade, which is A0's scope
     (C-005). FR-043.
2. **The language filter applies at read only**, on the effective reads; the activation document stays
   language-neutral. **A read with no activation applies no filtering.** Folded into FR-013 and FR-017 (PR B).
3. **A `suggests` edge's `when` surfaces as conditional** in the effective reads, above all the effective action
   context: an artifact reached only through `suggests` is listed as a suggestion carrying its `when` and `reason`.
   Artifacts reached through `requires` and the other hard relations stay plain entries. Folded into FR-013 and
   FR-019 (PR B and C).
