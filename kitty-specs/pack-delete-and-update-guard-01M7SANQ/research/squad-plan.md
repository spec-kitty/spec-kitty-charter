# Plan-point adversarial squad: findings and dispositions (2026-11-19)

The plan-point review was one independent reviewer agent, read-only, given the charter, the spec, the plan and the
code. It covered four lenses: correctness and concurrency, domain, contract, and plan gaps. Each finding was checked
against the code before its disposition. None was dropped.

| # | Sev | Finding (short) | Disposition |
|---|-----|-----------------|-------------|
| H1 | HIGH | Audit runs through `RegistrationAuditFilter`/`RegistrationPaths.classify`. It has no `DELETE /packs/{packId}` case and is blind to `dryRun`; a job records every end | **Folded** (P7 rewritten): `deletePack` is classified in `RegistrationPaths` and `WorktreeGuard`, like every attempt. A dry run is not recorded, on any of the three operations (NFR-002) |
| H2 | HIGH | `WholePackWriteStore.exclusively(String)` locks `writableRoot/<packId>` as written. A delete addressed by `pack_id` locks the wrong entry, and a listing's crash repair can resurrect the pack | **Folded** (P8): the uninstall goes through the `PackRemovalStore` seam, whose `exclusively(PackIdentifier)` resolves under the registry lock and locks the resolved directory (`exclusivelyAt`) |
| H3 | HIGH | "Move aside, then hide" leaves a `.pack-old-*` that the crash repair restores; a stale aside is also restored after a delete | **Folded** (P8): `hideStaleAsides` first, then one rename straight to `.pack-discard-*` under the generation bracket, then delete the tree; the generation counters are kept. A crash/restore case is added to `PackCommitStoreTest` |
| M1 | MED | A linked pack (#174): deleting its target leaves a dangling link, and `requireWritableLocation` answers 422 | **Folded** (P9): a linked pack is refused `409 registration_conflict` ("a linked pack is removed by removing its link"). The service never deletes outside the writable root |
| M2 | MED | The history is not healed before a delete; labels are compared as strings, so `1.1` and `1.1.0` slip through | **Folded** (P10): the stored pair is healed into the history before the removal, and label reuse is matched by PEP 440 equality (`compareVersions == 0`) |
| M3 | MED | The dependents fail open: an unreadable A has no `pack_id`, and unreadable others are skipped silently | **Folded** (P3): A's `pack_id` is salvaged from its `pack.yaml`. Any other configured pack whose descriptor cannot be read refuses the delete `500 pack_unreadable`, naming it (fail closed). Only direct dependents are listed. A pack that is both `depends_on` and `parent_pack` gives one violation per relation |

| M8 | MED | P2 opens several sessions; NFR-001 fails | **Folded** (P2): one `Session` is passed through the dependents, the inbound scan and `checkRemoval` |
