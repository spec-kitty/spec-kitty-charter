## Inherited from #554 (ADR-0075 slice 5, amendment of 2026-11-16)

Issue #554 built the checks this CLI runs, and left three pieces to it:

1. **The CLI entry over `PackCheck`.** `pack-validation`'s `PackCheck` is validate mode, the validation edge: it reads
   a pack once through the packs domain's `PackReading` port and returns a `PackVerdict` (the one read's verdict plus
   the placement rules', mapped by `PackVerdicts`). The CLI's `main` is the composition root that supplies
   `FileSystemPackInspector` as the `PackReading`, as `deployable` does for the service, and maps the verdict to the
   contract's problem JSON and the exit codes of ADR-0075 decision 9. `PACK_VALIDATION_IS_FRAMEWORK_FREE` and
   `ONION_DEPENDS_INWARD` let that `main` name the adapter, with a known-good control (the 2026-11-13 amendment).
2. **The pack-set check.** For `--config-root` or a repeated `--pack <dir>[:<level>]`: `PackCheck` per pack, then
   `PackRulesets.closure()` over the packs' composition facts, which the Spring-free builder makes from each pack's
   contents (no closure walk, ADR-0075 decision 7 as amended). The verdict names the checks that ran.
3. **The `pack-check` module merge.** `service/pack-check` stays a thin `main` over `pack-validation` (decision 9);
   whatever `pack-validation` holds for the CLI alone moves into it, so the jar carries no second copy of a check.
   Leaving Spring off the jar's runtime classpath, and proving it runs without it, stays this issue's.

Two things the CLI must settle that the service does not meet (pre-merge squad and acceptance, 2026-11-18):

- **The builder is not reachable yet.** `CompositionFactsBuilder` and its `underResolution` are package-private in
  `packs.infra.storage`. The pack-set check in item 2 needs a public entry, as an adapter the CLI's `main` names. It
  stays outside `packs.domain`.
- **Symlinks in a local directory.** The reader finds `pack.yaml`, the root shards and `drg/fragment.yaml` with
  `Files.isRegularFile`, which follows links. So a symlinked `pack.yaml` or shard is read from outside the pack root,
  with no finding; the census lists it as `LINK`, and no rule judges that. Archive uploads cannot carry links, so the
  service never meets this. A `pack-check` over a local directory does, and must decide whether a linked descriptor or
  shard is refused.
