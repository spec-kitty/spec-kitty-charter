# Authoring notes

Notes for the people who maintain this pack. They are not pack content: the service does not hold, serve or digest this
file, and a registration reports it as not held. The pack's own description is its `README.md`.

- Change a directive in `directives/` and raise `pack_version` in `pack.yaml` when the meaning changes.
- Keep a mission's orientation notes beside the mission; they are excluded the same way.
