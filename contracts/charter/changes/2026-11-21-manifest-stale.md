### Breaking

- A `pack-manifest.yaml` whose `content_hash` (of a constituent or a preset) or `manifest_hash` does not match the
  uploaded bytes is refused by whole-pack registration and validate mode under the new violation code `manifest_stale`,
  one per field, at the field in `pack-manifest.yaml` (`pack-manifest.yaml#/constituents/<n>/content_hash`,
  `#/presets/<n>/content_hash` or `#/manifest_hash`, the in-file pointer main `a18fb90f4` puts on the wire), its detail
  naming the field (`constituents{<path>}.content_hash`, `presets{<path>}.content_hash` or `manifest_hash`) (#630,
  operator ruling R-b; amends ADR-0042's amendment of 2026-11-12, under which the hashes were not judged and were
  restamped). It is judged with `manifest_membership`, after it and only when nothing else failed; a path
  `manifest_membership` names gets no `manifest_stale`. A correctly hashed manifest still registers and is restamped
  unchanged, and a per-artifact write still restamps the manifest. With `--round-trip`, `pack-doctor` no longer reports
  a source-stale manifest as `round_trip_red`: the cause is the one `manifest_stale`. Core's seam: an uploaded manifest
  carries the hashes ADR-0042 decision 5's recipes give, or is left out and generated by the service; a reader that
  matches the refusal by its code matches `manifest_stale`. Scenarios: `registration/whole-pack-registration.feature`,
  "An uploaded manifest with a stale content_hash is refused at the manifest, naming the field" and its control.
