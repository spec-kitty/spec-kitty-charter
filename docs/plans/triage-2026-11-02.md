# Issue triage, 2026-11-02

A triage pass over every open issue in this repository on 2026-11-02, the day the operator ruled the
pack-ecosystem design ([ADR-0053](/adr/0053-operator-rulings-on-the-pack-ecosystem-open-questions/),
[ADR-0054](/adr/0054-guards-are-enforced-checks-implemented-by-executable-assets/)). Each issue got a native type,
a proposed priority, a parent epic, a milestone and an action. The milestone definitions are in
[Milestones](/plans/milestones/), and the ordered pack-ecosystem work is in the
[work breakdown](/plans/pack-ecosystem-work-breakdown/).

## How the pass ran

- **Scope:** the 175 issues open at the start of the pass. Two issues filed during it (#349, #350) are listed
  under [Filed during the pass](#filed-during-the-pass) and were not triaged.
- **Method:** five planner subagents, each loaded with the planner profile and the charter's plan-action context,
  read their slice read-only against the ADRs and plans on `main` at `63b225c`. The orchestrator applied
  the mechanical changes centrally, after re-checking that no issue had closed or changed in the meantime.
- **Code baseline:** `origin/main` at `63b225c`. Claims that an issue is already fixed cite the PR, commit or test
  that is present on that ref.
- **Rulings after the pass:** `e1fe068` on `main` ruled ADR-0053 open questions 1 and 3 and ADR-0054 open
  question 3 after the table was drawn. The decision batch notes where they change a recommendation:
  issues #103, #112, #157 and #336.
- **Delivered after the pass:** PR #353 adds the procedure authoring checklist (#150, work-breakdown item 6) and
  documents one writer per pack (#183). Both issues close with it, so their rows below are already done.
- **Priority:** this repository has no `priority:*` labels, so priority is recorded here only. P1 means on the
  critical path of 4.0 GA, the 4.x cutover or the pack-ecosystem ordered work; P2 is planned work; P3 is backlog,
  a spike, or nice to have. No issue meets the P0 bar (a release-blocking defect).
