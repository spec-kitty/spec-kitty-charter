# Open questions

Each question names the default this repository works from until someone with the authority
decides. A ruling becomes an ADR here or in core; decided rows stay, marked with the date.

| 12 | **Where does first-load context state live?** The context read records first-load state in `.kittify/charter/context-state.json` and answers differently on a first load, so it is not a pure read. | **Decided 2026-10-19 (provisional):** it stays in core's seam; the service takes an integer `depth` parameter, echoed in the response, and stays a pure read model ([ADR-0011](/adr/0011-seam-runtime-fail-closed-daemon-and-freshness/)) | Operator |
| 13 | **Is a missing `pack_level` a refusal?** The service answered it with a non-blocking diagnostic, a call made without review. | **Decided 2026-10-18:** ratified as is. It stays a diagnostic, `pack_level_missing` ([`DiagnosticCode`](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-charter/blob/main/contracts/charter/schemas/DiagnosticCode.yaml)). The service is deliberately stricter than core: the diagnostic applies at every level and to a pack with no `pack.yaml`, and `pack_version` is required at every level. [spec-kitty-open-packs#47](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-open-packs/issues/47) brings every pack into line; [spec-kitty#6403](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty/issues/6403) asks core to correct its glossary, which calls it a refusal. **Settled in the schemas, 2026-10-19:** `pack_version` is required at every level, and `pack_level` is optional ([ADR-0017](/adr/0017-pack-schemas-imported-as-canonical-json-schema/), Amendment 2026-10-19; [DIV-004](/conformance/divergence-register/)) | Operator |
| 14 | **Which rules answer the action-scoped context?** The first slice was a call made without review. | **Decided 2026-10-19 (provisional):** [ADR-0018](/adr/0018-action-context-read-and-seam-access-surface/) answers it. The read is the offering's walk (scope from the action node, `requires`, `suggests` up to `depth`, the `requires` of the suggested artifacts, `vocabulary`), never filtered; `depth` counts `suggests` hops. Activation filtering is the effective-context operation of the activation contract set (R9, #103), which gates cutover stage 3. The differences from core are in the [divergence register](/conformance/divergence-register/). Still `x-provisional` until the conformance corpus ([#9](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-charter/issues/9)) decides them | Operator; then the corpus, #9 |
| 15 | **Where do the contract scenarios live?** The name `scenarios/` was a call made without review, forced by the terms guard. | **Decided 2026-10-18:** ratified and amended. `contracts/charter/scenarios/` keeps its name; only that subtree is on the suite's test classpath, and the suite runs the scenarios tagged `@contract`. Fixtures stay inline, and every scenario change gets a line in the contract's `CHANGELOG.md`. "Contract scenarios" is the canonical term | Operator |
| 16 | **How do the scenario suites fit together?** The repository has three homes for scenarios. | **Decided 2026-10-18:** each home stays, and moves or binds as follows ([Behavior and acceptance scenarios](/architecture/behavior-and-scenarios/#where-they-live)). Contract scenarios stay in `contracts/charter/scenarios/` and bind now. The pack registration suites of #43 (`src/test/resources/features/`) move to `contracts/charter/scenarios/<cluster>/` when their harness is bound, renamed for the terms guard, each cluster moved and bound in one change. The acceptance scenarios in `tests/scenarios/charter/` stay and bind with the harness of [#31](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-charter/issues/31) | Operator |
| 17 | **Who fixes the vendored-pack defects?** The `qa` pack has no `pack.yaml`, and the internal org pack requires glossary pack `spk-internal-glossary` while the vendored file stem is `spk-internal`. | **Owned upstream:** both are fixed in spec-kitty-open-packs ([#47](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-open-packs/issues/47) folds them in), then re-vendored here: the `qa` pack under [#36](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-charter/issues/36), the internal pack under [#37](https://github.com/spec-kitty/spec-kitty-charter/issues/37). No local fix | spec-kitty-open-packs, then this repository |

| 26 | **How does the service know who is calling?** The contract had no security scheme. | **Decided 2026-10-19 (provisional):** a required per-worktree bearer token on every operation, taken from the environment; startup is refused without one (at least 32 characters) unless `charter.insecure-no-token=true`; 401 and 403 are transport failures, not refusals.
