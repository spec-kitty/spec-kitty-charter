# 2026-11-19 — Tensions are a relation of their own and are answered by three reads

**A tension is the relation `tension`** (#602,
[ADR-0045](/adr/0045-tension-beside-the-relationships-and-nodes-keep-every-declared-field/), accepted 2026-11-18). Two
guidance artifacts in tension both stay valid; the charter now names, stores and serves that pull.

- **One spelling.** Packs write `tension`. `in_tension_with` is no longer read: a pack file that writes it is refused,
  and the content follows in spec-kitty-open-packs (#316).
- **One stored form.** A tension is written once, from the side its pack holds, or from the smaller URN when the pack
  holds both sides or neither. A pack that writes one twice, the other way round or from an artifact to itself is
  refused at load. A tension may sit beside one structural relationship on the same pair: they are two pair classes,
  and a registration or a removal touches only its own (`deletePackRelationship` takes `relation`).
- **In force.** A tension is in force when both sides are. When the authoring pack's own version of a side loses the
  layer merge, its tension is gone; a side it does not hold resolves to the version in force.
- **Three reads.** The effective graph answers each tension in force once, as a `tension` edge with its first writer's
  `when` and `reason` and `reconciledBy`, the artifacts in force that reconcile both sides. The action context answers
  `tensions`, those whose two sides it lists in force. `getActivationTensions` answers every tension in force and one
  `unreconciled_tension` finding for each that nothing reconciles, offering `reconcile` and, when the exclusion write
  would accept a side, `deactivate_one_side` with a link to that write. A finding never refuses.
- **Breaking.** The required `tensions` member and the edge's new members break a client with closed schemas, and the
  effective tags move to `v4`, so each stored tag answers `200` once. The contract change fragments list each item;
  the differences from core are DIV-108 and DIV-615 to DIV-618.
