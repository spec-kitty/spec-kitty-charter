# 2026-10-29 — ADR-0041 and ADR-0042 are accepted

**Two records are ratified.** On the operator's ruling (Stijn Dejongh, 2026-10-29),
[ADR-0041](/adr/0041-containerized-configuration-model/) (the containerized service's configuration
model) and [ADR-0042](/adr/0042-pack-level-files-sanitized-shapes-and-the-derived-manifest/)
(pack-level files, sanitized shapes and the derived manifest) move from **Proposed** to
**Accepted**. Each keeps its original proposal date of 2026-10-28 as a parenthetical, and the
forward and back references from neighboring records drop the "proposed" qualifier. Nothing on the
wire or in the build changes; only the status of the two records does.

The [ADR index](/adr/) records the new statuses.
