# Reader persona: Governance lead

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| **Group** | outside this repository, adopts what it produces |
| **Actor type** | human |
| **Primary goal** | Let people make the calls and have agents build with what the team decided. |

## What this reader needs

- A plain answer to what the charter does: it resolves the packs a project draws from into
  the guidance for one action, so that agents act on the team's agreed rules.
- The vocabulary of packs, levels, activation and presets, without reading the schemas.
- To see that a rule authored in an org pack reaches the agent, or fails loudly when it
  cannot.
- What is designed and what exists today, stated once and honestly.

## What frustrates them

- Governance that is written, validated and then silently dropped before an agent sees it.
- Terms that change meaning between pages.

## Signal that the documentation served them

A lead can explain to their team what adopting the charter changes, which decisions are
theirs (the packs and activations) and which are the platform's.
